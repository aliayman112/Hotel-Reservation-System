Help & Support

Answers to common questions about searching, booking, and managing stays.

How do I cancel a booking?

Go to My Bookings and select Cancel next to any confirmed reservation.

Why is my booking still pending?

Reservations are reviewed by the hotel admin before being confirmed or rejected.

Can I change my dates after booking?

Not yet — cancel the current booking and submit a new reservation with the new dates.

About Book & Stay

A simple way to find and reserve rooms across the country.

Book & Stay connects travelers with hotels in Cairo, Alexandria, Giza, Luxor, Aswan, Hurghada, Sharm El Sheikh, and Dahab. Search by destination and dates, review room options, and submit a reservation request — the hotel confirms availability and you're ready to go.
